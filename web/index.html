02 · Behind you

The tape they feel
before they see.

BigVelo is Garmin Varia™ rear radar compatible. Cars coming up behind you land on a live strip we call the tape — green when they’re far, amber as they close, red when they’re close. It shows their speed. Your Watch buzzes. The tape follows you between screens.

  • Live vehicle range and closing speed on the edge of every page
  • Watch haptics plus tones that duck your music — single or multi
  • Park the tape left, right, top or bottom. The numbers stay huge.

* BigVelo is not affiliated with Garmin.
