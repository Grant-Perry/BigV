Help

Support

Write us at info@cre8vplanet.com. Say which device, iOS version, and what the cockpit was doing. We read it.

Trial

Thirty days from first launch on that device. Resetting onboarding does not move the clock. When it ends, you can still open past rides, saved routes and export. Recording, live radar and Watch start wait on a purchase.

Monthly, yearly, lifetime

Buy in the app through StoreKit. Restore if you switched phones. Redeem codes from Settings. Cancel a subscription in your Apple ID subscriptions list — we cannot cancel it for you.

Radar

BigVelo speaks the Varia™ rear-radar protocol. Pair from Rear Radar in Settings. The tape is an overlay so the speed hero does not shrink. Placement is left, right, top or bottom. We are not Garmin and not a replacement for looking over your shoulder.

Watch

Install the BigVelo Watch app. Heart rate comes from the Watch. Start, pause and end are pinned. If the glance is stale, check that iPhone and Watch are talking and the phone ride is live.

Routes and GPX

Ride To uses Apple’s cycling directions. Import GPX from the Route tab. Export GPX from a finished ride’s story. A BigVelo backup is rides plus preferences — not a file another cycling app will open as a single track. Use GPX for that.
