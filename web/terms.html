The product

BigVelo is cycling software for iPhone and Apple Watch. It can record rides, show a Garmin Varia™-compatible rear radar tape, plan and follow bike routes, speak turns, detect climbs, show weather, and keep a ride story you can export. It is a computer on the bars — not a substitute for looking at the road, lights, bells, or your own judgment.

Safety

Radar, maps, spoken turns and weather are aids. They can be late, wrong, or silent. Ride as if they are not there. BigVelo is not affiliated with Garmin. Varia is a trademark of Garmin Ltd. Apple, iPhone, Apple Watch, HealthKit and WeatherKit are trademarks of Apple Inc.

Trial and payment

Thirty days from first launch on a device are the full cockpit. After that, starting a ride, live radar and Watch start require an App Store purchase: monthly, yearly, or lifetime. Prices are what the App Store shows in your storefront. Subscriptions renew until you cancel in your Apple ID settings. The lifetime product is a one-time non-consumable. Past rides remain available to view and export if the trial ends and you have not purchased.

Refunds and billing disputes are handled by Apple under App Store rules. Restore Purchases and offer-code redeem are in the app.

Your data
